One-time Puter token

Puter is a free AI service (ai.google-free style, via puter.com) with a monthly allowance and 1000+ models. This app uses it server-side, so it needs a one-time token from your Puter account.

  1. 1. Click Get my token below — Puter opens its sign-in popup (it may be blocked; allow popups for this page).
  2. 2. In the popup, sign in, create a free account, or continue as guest, and approve the app.
  3. 3. Copy the token below and paste it into .env.local as PUTER_AUTH_TOKEN=…, then restart the server.

Ready.
